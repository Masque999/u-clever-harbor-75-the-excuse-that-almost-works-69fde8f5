# The Excuse That Almost Works

One line, at most 140 characters and 20 words: the excuse a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week.

Entry: `My flight was delayed and my laptop died, but the report is finished—it's just on the laptop that died.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
